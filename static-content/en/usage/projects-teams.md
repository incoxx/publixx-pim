# Projects & Teams

Use **Projects** and **Teams** to organise collaboration on product data. Teams group users; projects group time-bound initiatives with owners, teams and assigned products.

## Teams

A team is a group of users that can be assigned to several projects.

1. Navigation: **Project Management → Organisation → Teams**.
2. Create a **"New Team"** — set name, description and members.
3. The team is then available for assignment in projects.

## Projects

A project organises an initiative with start/end dates, a responsible person (manager), assigned teams and products, and a status (*Planning, Active, Completed, Paused*).

1. Navigation: **Project Management → Organisation → Projects**.
2. Create a **"New Project"** — name, optional description, start and end date.
3. Assign a manager, optionally assign teams.
4. Assign products (bulk assignment is possible).
5. Set the status and save. The list shows the number of assigned teams and products per project.

## Project Dashboard

Under **Project Management → Project Dashboard**, several widgets visualise the state of work:

- **Project timeline** — chronological view of projects
- **Team workload** — workload per team
- **Workflow status** — status distribution
- **Active projects** — list of ongoing projects

## Permissions

Projects and teams are governed by their own rights (view, create, edit, delete). Assignment is under [Roles & Permissions](/en/administration/roles).
